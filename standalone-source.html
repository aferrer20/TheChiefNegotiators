01Buy-Side · Procurement

I act as your senior procurement advisor on major deals — vendor contracts, SaaS renewals, capital purchases, professional services. I sit beside you (or take it entirely) and make sure the other side doesn't walk out with the better number.

02Manufacturer Lock-Ins

Beat the next price increase letter.

Manufacturers raise prices on a calendar. I lock in fixed-term, fixed-price agreements before the letter lands — protecting margin for 12 to 36 months while your competitors absorb the hit.

  • Volume & tier negotiation
  • Force majeure & index clauses
  • Multi-supplier hedging

Spinning up a new division, partner program, or channel ecosystem? I've done it from inside startups and for operators who don't have time to figure it out twice. Org design, partner economics, GTM architecture — handed off, not hung up.

  • Partner / channel program design
  • Compensation & partner economics
  • Founding-team operating cadence

How we handle what you tell us.

Every mandate starts with information a competitor would want — requirement, budget, timeline, sometimes an existing proposal. What follows is how that stays where it belongs.

Confidentiality by design

Nothing about your position leaves the room.

This is structural, not a policy we point to. Holding no inventory and taking no position in any deal means there is nothing to gain by moving your information without you.

  • —
    Anonymous first contact

    You can open a conversation, and even request the offtaker qualification instrument, before your organization is named.

  • —
    MNDA on request

    Available before anything specific about your requirement, budget or timeline is discussed.

  • —
    Nothing circulated without consent

    Your requirement, pricing floor and commercial position are not shown to a counterparty until you have agreed to the introduction.

  • —
    Sensitive instruments are gated, not published

    The offtaker qualification brief records identity, load profile and commitment position — it is issued on request to a named person, not posted for anyone to download.

Counterparty & compliance screening

Every introduction is screened before it is made.

This runs both ways — on buyers before we bring a requirement to an operator, and on operators before we bring demand to them.

01

Ultimate offtaker identified

We confirm the party whose workload actually occupies the capacity and whose balance sheet stands behind the contract, not an intermediary assembling a position to flip.

02

Ownership verified

Legal entity and beneficial ownership are confirmed before a file moves forward.

03

Sanctions & AML screening

Run on every mandate, worked through alongside your compliance or legal team where one is involved, before anything reaches a credit committee.

How the website handles data

What we collect, and what we do not.

  • —
    Forms ask for what qualifies you

    Contact and requirement details needed to route and respond to your inquiry — nothing sold, nothing marketed against beyond that response.

  • —
    Third-party delivery, not third-party ownership

    Inquiry forms are transmitted through Web3Forms, a form-processing service, for delivery to our inbox. It is a mail pipe, not a party we share your information with for any other purpose.

  • —
    Analytics is minimized

    We use Google Analytics with IP anonymization enabled to understand which pages are useful. It is not used to build a profile on you, and we do not sell or share this data with third parties.

  • —
    No public requirement database

    What you tell us during qualification is not stored in anything browsable, and it is not repurposed for a different mandate without asking you first.

Questions

Asked before signing anything.

01

Do you sell or share the information I submit?

+

No. Information you share is used only to evaluate and run your mandate. It is not sold, rented or shared with third parties for marketing purposes, and it is not circulated to a counterparty without your consent.

02

Is my inquiry confidential before anything is signed?

+

Yes. Anonymous first contact is fine, an MNDA is available before anything specific is discussed, and nothing about your position — identity, requirement or commercial terms — leaves the firm without your written consent.

03

Do you hold SOC 2, ISO 27001 or a similar certification?

+

Not at this time. As an independent advisory of our size, confidentiality is enforced through minimal data collection, restricted internal access and contractual confidentiality (MNDA) rather than a third-party audit framework. If a specific certification is a requirement of your vendor onboarding process, raise it during qualification and we will address it directly.

04

What screening runs before an introduction is made?

+

For every mandate we identify the ultimate offtaker, verify ownership, and run sanctions and AML screening before an introduction is made on either side — worked through alongside your compliance team where one is involved.

05

What happens to my information if we don't move forward?

+

It stays with the firm and is not circulated. We do not maintain a public database of requirements or positions, and nothing you shared during qualification is repurposed for another mandate without asking you first.

A specific requirement for your onboarding process?

Tell us what your compliance or procurement team needs to see and we will address it directly, before anything is signed.

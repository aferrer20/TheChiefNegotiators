Questions

Asked about the board.

01

Why are rates, sites and dates not published?

+

They are commercially sensitive to the parties behind the builds, and publishing them would anchor a market that moves weekly. Indicative pricing sits in a $3–5 per GPU-hour band, set primarily by term length. Exact figures come back within one business day of a qualified enquiry.

02

Do you own or resell any of this?

+

No. We hold no inventory and we are not the seller. You contract directly with the supplier, and we negotiate alongside you.

03

Is a deposit really required?

+

Yes, on every build — 30% at three years, 20–25% at five, because a longer commitment de-risks the supplier. Nothing is held without one, so the matcher will not show builds to a party who cannot place one.

04

What does "in motion" mean?

+

The build has a committed service date and is already underway. Faster to stand up than an open specification, with correspondingly less room to change the configuration.

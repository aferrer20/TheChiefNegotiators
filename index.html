The Model

How we are structured, and why it keeps us honest.

Everyone in this market is paid by someone. What matters is whether that changes the advice. Ours is structured so it does not, and the reasons are below.

01

Alignment

Never paid out of your deal.

Winning business carries a cost of sale that exists whether or not we are involved. That is where we are paid from, so our involvement is funded by the transaction rather than added to it.

02

Independence

We buy nothing and sell nothing.

No inventory, no listings, no resale position, no stake in what gets signed — so nothing we put in front of you is something we need you to take. What we provide is access: to capacity, to allocation, to power, and to the parties on the other side. You contract directly with the counterparty and own that relationship outright.

03

Whole-stack assembly

We can bring the deal, not one piece of it.

A complete AI infrastructure deal usually needs three things that live in three different places: an offtaker with real demand, energised capacity that fits it, and compute to fill it. Normally that is three separate searches run by parties who never meet. We hold relationships at every layer, so we can assemble the pieces into one transaction — and every party at the table knows we are working every part of it.

04

Direct access

Straight to the person who decides.

Relationships at every layer of this market already exist, so a requirement reaches the person who can actually say yes rather than entering a process. Nothing has to be circulated to find them, which is why a mandate can run to completion without your position becoming common knowledge.

05

Continuity

We stay after the signature.

The engagement does not end at the close. We keep advising as the market moves and bring you what is coming next, so you are early to the opportunity rather than late to it.

06

Discretion

Confidential by default.

Anonymous at first contact, MNDA on request, and nothing about your position leaves the room.

Coverage

Where we run mandates.

Capacity is a local problem with a global market. Power price, permitting speed, latency requirement and tax treatment all move by region, so the right answer is rarely the market nearest you.

North America

Northern Virginia, Dallas–Fort Worth and ERCOT, Phoenix, Atlanta, Chicago, Columbus, plus secondary markets where power is available sooner.

Nordics

Iceland, Norway and Sweden — low-cost renewable power, free cooling, and the strongest cost-per-megawatt case in Europe for training workloads.

UK & Ireland

London and Slough for latency-bound inference; Ireland where grid position allows it.

Continental Europe

Frankfurt, Amsterdam and Paris for the FLAP-D core, with Nordic and Iberian alternatives when FLAP-D power is constrained.

APAC

Singapore, Johor and Malaysia, and Japan — where the Singapore moratorium legacy has pushed capacity across the strait.

Latin America

Brazil, Chile and Mexico — hydro and renewable-heavy grids with improving connectivity and a favourable cost basis.

Before You Reach Out

Worth knowing first.

Answered here so the first call can be about your deal instead of about us. The first one is the question we get most.

01

You work with buyers and with operators. How does that work?

+

Often we work all of it at once, and that is the point.

A complete AI infrastructure deal usually needs three things that live in three different places — an offtaker with real demand, energised capacity that fits it, and compute to fill it. Normally those are three separate searches, run by three parties who never meet, on three different timelines. Because we hold relationships at every layer, we can assemble them into a single transaction: bring qualified demand to an operator, bring capacity to a buyer, bring GPUs to either.

Nobody is being played off against anybody. Every party at the table knows we are working every part of it, and we say so at the outset — disclosure is the mechanism, not exclusivity.

What keeps it straight is that we hold no inventory and take no position in the outcome. We do not earn more from one configuration than another, so the only version of a deal we have any reason to push is the one that actually closes and still makes sense in year three.

02

What are we actually hiring you for?

+

The ecosystem, and the speed it gives you. We already know who leads in each part of this market and how to reach the person who can actually decide, so you spend your time on the right conversations instead of finding them.

03

What does this cost?

+

For buy-side mandates, nothing directly — winning business carries a cost of sale that exists whether or not we are involved, and that is where we are paid from. For operators, we are a small line in your operating cost and we work as an extension of your team, doing the vetting, the qualifying and the negotiation so that what reaches your desk is ready to execute.

04

Who pays you, and how does that not distort the advice?

+

The ecosystem does. We hold no inventory and take no position in the outcome, so nothing we bring forward is something we need anyone to take.

The honest answer to why that holds is not a values statement, it is arithmetic: we are only useful to this market for as long as both sides keep trusting us. One deal where we bent a number to close it costs us every deal after it. Reputation is the only collateral we have.

05

Is it too early to talk to you?

+

No. The earlier we are involved, the more room there is to shape the terms rather than react to them. A first conversation commits you to nothing and often just clarifies what you are actually looking at.

06

How fast can you move?

+

First response within one business day. The relationships are already in place, so we are not starting a search from zero, and for qualified offtake we work from an existing bench of vetted demand.

07

Which regions do you cover?

+

North America, the Nordics, the UK and Ireland, continental Europe including Frankfurt, Amsterdam and Paris, APAC including Singapore, Japan and Malaysia, and Latin America. Full coverage detail is above.

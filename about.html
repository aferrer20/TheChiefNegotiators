Founder

The person behind the desk.

Amanda Ferrer founded The Chief Negotiators as an independent buy-side advisory desk for enterprise AI infrastructure. Her work spans GPU cluster procurement, data center capacity sourcing and DC power — operating on behalf of the end user, not the supply side. Every mandate is run with the same principle: access is earned through integrity, not volume.

Prior to founding TCN, Amanda served as Co-Founder and Chief Revenue Officer at Strategic Supply Partners, where she built sourcing relationships across the data center and GPU supply chain. That network — operators, OEM channel partners, power developers and site owners — is the foundation this firm operates from.

The Desk

An expert boutique, not a marketplace.

The Chief Negotiators is a boutique advisory firm. There is one desk, one standard, and no inventory. Every mandate is opened by an end user — the party whose workload runs on the hardware and whose balance sheet stands behind the contract.

Buy-side only

We act for the buyer. Compensation is disclosed at the start of every mandate, funded by the transaction, and never contingent on which operator or platform you choose.

No inventory held

We do not buy, store or sell capacity. Every position you see through this desk exists with an operator or OEM channel partner — we surface it, benchmark it, and negotiate it on your behalf.

End users only

Brokers and resellers can name an end user and work with us — once the offtaker is named and verified. Anonymous chains and paper positions are not something we participate in.

Integrity as access

Operators quote us real numbers because they trust how we represent demand. That trust is the entire product. Protecting it is not a policy — it is how this firm stays useful to both sides of the market.

Ready to open a file?

Tell us what you are trying to build. We'll take it from there.

Anonymous first contact is fine. An MNDA is available before anything specific is discussed. First response within one business day.
